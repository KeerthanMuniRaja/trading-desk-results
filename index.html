What the models are taught

Classic trading tips are taught to the models only after they are checked on our own data: 17 of 25 hold, 0 are contradicted, 8 have no check yet.

  • Four of our eight shares are banks. Over the last ~2 years the banks' daily moves correlate 0.47 with each other, against 0.26 with the other shares.
  • ITC and NTPC usually fall less than the banks. Betas to the basket over ~2 years: ITC 0.72, NTPC 0.9; the banks average 1.08.
  • Trading costs decide small edges. Our model charges 0.15% on each unit traded, one way: a full monthly rebalance in and out costs about 0.3% of the money moved.
  • After SEVERE distribution days, falls are deeper. After SEVERE days the next 20 sessions' average worst fall was -3.58%, after NORMAL days -2.03%.
  • The best backtest of the past rarely stays best. Picking, each year, the idea with the best record so far and holding it the next year: 29 months ahead of the basket and 41 behind out of sample, -0.421% a month on average.
  • Small positions pay a fixed charge on every sale. Selling a third of a Rs 5,000 budget (Rs 1,666) pays a Rs 15.93 depository charge: 0.96% of the sale, on top of the 0.15% percentage costs.
